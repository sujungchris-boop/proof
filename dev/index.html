About PROOF

Chris & Partners has organized conventions and corporate events in Seoul for fifteen years. PROOF is the part of the company that works only on Web3: the side events, summits and dinners that cluster around Korea Blockchain Week and the global conferences.

Most of our clients are foreign teams who need things to work in Seoul without learning the city first, and Korean teams who need the same thing in Singapore or Bangkok. We hold the venue and vendor relationships, read the contracts, and are in the room on the night.
